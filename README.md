# GenAI MasterMind

**Master Generative AI — from concepts to implementation.**

An interactive, browser-based course covering Generative AI through 40 hands-on guides, quizzes, build labs, and a 70-question final exam.

---

## What you'll learn

Seven modules plus a final exam — work through them in order.

| # | Module | What you'll cover |
|---|--------|-------------------|
| 1 | **Fundamentals** | What GenAI is, how it differs from traditional AI, embeddings, safety, evaluation *(10 guides)* |
| 2 | **Prompt Engineering** | CO-STAR and META frameworks, prompting techniques *(3 guides)* |
| 3 | **Context Engineering** | Context windows, memory, retrieval foundations *(3 guides)* |
| 4 | **MCP (Model Context Protocol)** | Fundamentals and advanced patterns *(3 guides)* |
| 5 | **Architectures** | LLM chat, RAG, workflows, agents, agentic AI *(7 guides)* |
| 6 | **Role Labs** | PM lab and citizen-coder lab *(3 guides)* |
| 7 | **Build Labs & Capstone** | RAG, workflows, single-agent, multi-agent, capstone *(6 guides)* |
| — | **Final Exam** | 70-question assessment across all modules |

Each module ends with a short quiz. The final exam is your capstone.

---

## How to use

1. Open [`index.html`](./index.html) in any modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge).
2. Pick a module and click any guide to start.
3. Each guide has a sidebar of numbered steps on the left — work through them top to bottom.
4. Use the prev/next arrows in the top right to move between guides.
5. Finish all seven modules, then take the final exam.

No install, no setup, no account. Just open and learn.

### Tips for getting the most out of it

- **Do the interactions.** Every guide has try-it-yourself moments — drag, sort, classify, decide. Don't just read past them.
- **Take the quizzes twice.** Once to test yourself, once to reinforce what you missed.
- **Work in order.** Later modules assume you've done the earlier ones.
